Add this ONE rule to the mtnshade.com Netlify site (top of its _redirects file, or netlify.toml).
It makes mtnshade.com/customize/ show the Mug Studio, which lives in its own Netlify site.
Replace mtnshade-studio with your studio site's actual Netlify name.

--- _redirects ---
/customize/*  https://mtnshade-studio.netlify.app/:splat  200!

--- or netlify.toml ---
[[redirects]]
  from = "/customize/*"
  to = "https://mtnshade-studio.netlify.app/:splat"
  status = 200
  force = true

Also add a Netlify env var on the STUDIO site: PUBLIC_URL = https://mtnshade.com/customize
